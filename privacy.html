Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

LUMÉ is a skin analysis and personal progress app operated by Ahmed Tahir ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information LUMÉ collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it in plain English.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Your password is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider and is never visible to us.

Face photos. The core of LUMÉ is a face scan. When you scan, the app captures three photos of your face (front, right side, left side) using your camera, with your permission. These photos are uploaded to your private account storage and are used solely to generate your personal skin analysis and scores. They are not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, not used to identify you across other services, and not used to train third-party systems.

Scores and analysis. We store the skin scores and category results derived from your scans so you can track your progress over time.

Self-reported wellbeing information. LUMÉ lets you log daily lifestyle information such as water intake, sleep, skincare habits, movement, and free-text notes about your skin, meals, or mood. This is self-reported lifestyle information you choose to enter; it is not clinical or medical data, and LUMÉ does not provide medical advice.

Onboarding answers. Your answers to the onboarding questionnaire (for example habits, self-perception, and goals) are stored to personalise your experience.

Subscription status. We record whether your account has an active subscription, which plan, and its expiry, so the app can unlock your features. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your card details.

2. Face Data

What we collect. When you complete a scan, LUMÉ captures three photos of your face (front, left side and right side) using your iPhone's front camera. Apple's Vision framework analyses these photos on your device to detect facial landmark points, which LUMÉ uses to calculate measurements such as symmetry and proportion and to produce your LUMÉ Score. We call the photos, the landmark points and the measurements derived from them "face data".

How we use it. Face data is used only to calculate your scores and to show your own progress over time inside the app. It is never used to identify you, to authenticate you, for advertising, for tracking, or to train any machine learning or AI model. LUMÉ does not use ARKit or the TrueDepth camera. LUMÉ does offer an optional Face ID app lock in Settings; this uses your iPhone's own Face ID system to unlock the app and does not give LUMÉ any access to your face image or to the face data described here.

Sharing. We do not sell, share, rent or disclose face data to any third party. Face photos are stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes them only on our behalf to provide the app. No other service provider, including RevenueCat, Apple or any affiliate partner, receives face data.

Storage. Face photos are stored in a private, access-controlled storage bucket hosted by Supabase in London, United Kingdom. Only your signed-in account can access them, through temporary links that expire within one hour. Landmark points are processed on your device and are not stored; the measurements derived from them are stored with your account.

Retention and deletion. We keep face data only for as long as your account exists, so you can compare scans over time. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data, including every face photo and measurement, at any time in Settings > Delete my account. Deletion removes this data from our storage immediately. You can also contact us at tahirtariq0195@gmail.com to request deletion.

3. What we do not collect

LUMÉ contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers. We do not collect your location, contacts, or microphone audio. We do not use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. How your information is used

Your information is used to: create and secure your account; generate your skin analysis, scores, and product matches; show your history and progress; operate your subscription; and respond to support requests. That's it.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to run LUMÉ, acting on our instructions:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and private photo storage.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription entitlements. RevenueCat receives an anonymised user ID and purchase/transaction events from Apple; it does not receive your photos or wellbeing data.
  • Apple — processes all payments and manages your subscription billing.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure provided by the services above. Face photos are held in private storage accessible only to your authenticated account.

7. How long we keep your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. If you delete your account, your photos, scores, logs, answers, and account records are deleted from our systems, including your stored face photos.

8. Deleting your account and your rights

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Settings → Delete Account). This permanently removes your account and the data described above.

If you are in the UK or EU, you have rights under data protection law including access, correction, deletion, restriction, and portability of your personal data, and the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK. To exercise any right, contact us at the address below.

9. Subscriptions and cancellation

Subscriptions are purchased and billed through your Apple ID and can be cancelled at any time in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

10. Age

LUMÉ is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update this page and the date above. Continued use of LUMÉ after changes means you accept the updated policy.
